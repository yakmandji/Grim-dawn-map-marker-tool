How to use

Help

You can select a map from the dropdown under the minimap (main or DLC)

Markers

  • Select new marker and the categorie. Give a name to your marker and click in the map
  • For each marker you create, you can edit it, delete it, get a shareable link, or move it to the completed panel

Notes

  • You can add region notes by hovering over region names

Routes

  • Select new route and click on the map for the first point. Then continue to draw
  • Presse space to move the map while drawing a route. Press escape or the finish button to finish route

Share

  • Click Share map to generate a link that contain all your markers, routes and notes
  • Share your journey and quest notes, and contribute to a richer community map.

Tools

  • Delete all markers, Routes or notes with the tools section will delete all items but only from the active character
